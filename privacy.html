Vela Tennis Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Vela Tennis is made by Marcelo Zapata. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

Vela Tennis does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Camera and movement analysis

The movement analysis feature uses your device's camera to analyze your body movement and swing timing. This analysis happens entirely on your device. Video and images are not uploaded, saved to our servers, or shared with anyone. The camera is used only when you choose that feature, and only after you grant permission. You can turn camera access off at any time in your device's Settings.

Data stored on your device

Information you create in the app, such as your avatar, outfit choices, and match journal entries, stays on your device. We do not receive a copy of it.

Links to other sites

Some screens link to outside websites or maps, such as official venue sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
