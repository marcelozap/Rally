What this is. Pose estimation running in your browser. It finds 33 points on your body in each frame and computes angles between them. Everything you see is trigonometry on those points.

What it isn't. It does not diagnose anything and it does not know why something hurts. There is no single correct way to move — good form varies with your anatomy, and the useful comparison is against your own numbers over time, not against a template. If something hurts, these numbers are worth showing to a physio. They are not a substitute for one.

Accuracy. Depth from one camera is a guess, so everything here is measured in 2D. Keep the camera in the same place between sessions or the sessions aren't comparable.
