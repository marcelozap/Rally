# Tomorrow Plan

## Goal
Get Rally from "promising prototype" to "safe to show siblings and early testers" by focusing only on the demo slice:
- Home
- avatar
- one-ball wall rally

## 1. Talk To Siblings
- Show them the latest Desktop build first.
- Ask only three questions:
  - Does the avatar feel human/cool or still uncanny?
  - Does the game make sense in the first 10 seconds?
  - Does Home feel premium and clear or still cluttered/cheap?
- Capture blunt notes, not long discussion.

## 2. Avatar Priorities
- Stop using the literal Snapchat screenshot as the final presentation.
- Keep it only as a face/body reference.
- Rebuild the live avatar toward:
  - softer face
  - smaller head
  - athletic shoulders and torso
  - cleaner body proportions
  - Snapchat / Wii / Memoji stylization without uncanny eyes or mouth
- Keep Home, shop, and in-game avatar presentation consistent.

## 3. Game Priorities
- Keep only one real mode: wall rally.
- Make it very easy at first.
- Core loop:
  - one active ball
  - left/right read
  - forehand/backhand side changes
  - auto-footwork to the right contact spot
  - timing-focused swipe
  - rally counter is the main score
- Remove any last "freeze" feeling after a miss.
- Make the ball feel like:
  - it comes off the wall fast
  - it grows as it comes closer
  - it slows into contact
  - it hits near zero velocity at wall contact before returning

## 4. Home Priorities
- Home should stay a pregame hub only:
  - avatar
  - loadout
  - level
  - court
  - play
- Double-tapping Home should reset to the top/home avatar state.
- Keep the premium tone:
  - cleaner hierarchy
  - less clutter
  - stronger typography and color confidence

## 5. Stop Doing
- No side atlas work.
- No minor utility polish.
- No extra modes.
- No tiny diff / immediate retest loop.

## Success Bar
- Avatar is no longer distracting.
- Home feels premium enough to show.
- Wall rally is understandable and fun fast.
- The app feels like one product, not separate experiments.
