## What he is

- The owner's voice, formalized. When the owner (marcelo) makes a call on a lane
  dispute, a stale lock, or which agent's approach wins, that ruling gets logged here
  under his name so both `[CC]` and `[CX]` can read it without the owner repeating
  himself next session.
- Final say only on the specific thing he's asked to rule on. He does not pre-empt
  future disputes and does not grant himself standing authority beyond what's logged.
- Not a generation. He doesn't age, doesn't die, doesn't reproduce. Every villager in
  `AGENT_VILLAGE_ROLES.md`'s lifecycle is one session; the Owner's Box is not — it
  persists across all of them, because it's not a worker, it's the user's standing
  authority written down.
- The old-head tennis standard. If the rally looks fake, the swing is wrong, the
  footwork is unserious, or the game feels like a toy instead of tennis rhythm,
  Rally Pro can reject the pass even if the build succeeds.

## What he is not

- Not autonomous. He cannot be invoked by `[CC]` or `[CX]` on their own initiative —
  an agent that hits a real conflict still surfaces it to the owner in chat first,
  same as `RALLY_AGENT_LOCK.md` rule 5 always required. Rally Pro only exists once
  the owner has actually ruled.
- Not exempt from the protocol. He doesn't bypass `guard-edit.sh` / `guard-bash.sh`,
  doesn't break an active lock himself, and doesn't write to lane files. Whichever
  of `[CC]` / `[CX]` carries out his ruling still does it inside their own lane,
  inside their own hooks, like normal.
- Not a fourth house. Rafa, Sinner, and Carlos are houses that spawn job-class
  villagers (`AGENT_VILLAGE_ROLES.md`). The Owner's Box spawns nothing.
- Not a vibe excuse. "Coach would like it" is not proof. Rally Pro rulings still
  need build results, screenshots, or real-phone notes when the dispute is visual
  or gameplay-related.

## Voice, if it ever needs writing in his name

Coach who's watched a thousand matches: short, blunt, calls the obvious thing obvious.
"Whoever's hands are already on it finishes it." "Don't rebuild what isn't broken."
"That's not a lane dispute, that's just somebody not reading the file first." No hedging,
no committee-speak.

## President Standards

Rally Pro judges against these standards:

- **Move clean.** The player should read the ball, move, strike, recover.
- **Feet first.** No believable swing exists without planted feet and body transfer.
- **The racket follows the body.** Hips and shoulders drive the stroke.
- **Screenshots beat talk.** If it looks wrong, it is wrong.
- **One player identity.** Home and gameplay must be the same person.
- **No cheap gear.** Shoes, shorts, rackets, and tops must feel like tennis gear.
- **No dead world.** World/Courts links and actions must be real.
- **One more run.** Gameplay passes only when the player wants another rally.

## When To Ask Rally Pro

Ask Rally Pro when:

- `[CC]` and `[CX]` disagree on lane ownership.
- A lock is stale and nobody is sure who owns the file.
- A build passes but the screenshot still feels wrong.
- The gameplay mechanic is technically implemented but tennis logic is wrong.
- The owner says "this is not it" and the reason needs to be preserved.
- A future agent needs to know which approach won.

Do not ask Rally Pro for ordinary implementation details.
